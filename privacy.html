Privacy

Crucible runs entirely on your computer. It has no account system, makes no network requests, and collects no telemetry, analytics or crash reports. Nothing you type, log or attach ever leaves your PC unless you export it yourself.

Your data lives in %APPDATA%\dev.armaan.crucible\projects\ as plain JSON files plus the screenshots you attach. Uninstalling the app does not delete that folder; delete it yourself if you want it gone.

The optional Claude connector (crucible-mcp.cjs) reads and writes those same files on your machine. What Claude sees of your project depends on the tools you ask it to use, and is governed by your own Claude account's terms, not by Crucible.

The website you are reading is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub's own privacy policy applies to page views there.
